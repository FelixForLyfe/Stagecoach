# Player guide

Everything you need to ride Stagecoach's roads: building a coach, finding stops, paying the fare and what happens on
the way.

## In five steps

1. **Find a village.** Every village has a coach stop, built in the village's own wood.
2. **Right-click the coach** to discover the stop. Dust kicks up, the coachman sounds his horn and a road sign shows
   the stop's name.
3. **Right-click a stop you know** to open the destination list: every stop you've discovered, with its distance,
   fare and travel time.
4. **Pick a destination and travel.** You pay the fare (a few XP levels, or items on some servers) and you're on the
   road. Watch it roll past until you arrive.
5. **Build your own.** Craft a stagecoach, place it wherever you want a stop and give it a name.
