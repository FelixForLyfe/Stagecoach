# Your options

## Stagecoach Options

Each player has their own options. Open them with the **Config** button next to Stagecoach in the Mods list (Forge and
NeoForge) or in Mod Menu (Fabric).

| Option | On by default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Simplified loading screen | no | While travelling, show a map with both stops and your marker instead of the road scene |
| Minimal loading screen | no | While travelling, show only the sign, the progress bar and Cancel; wins over the map |
| Other stops on the map | yes | Mark the other stagecoaches you know as small dots on the travel map |
| Placement preview | yes | Show a ghost of the coach where it would go while you hold one |
| Discovery effects | yes | Road dust, lantern embers and a road-sign card when you discover a stop |
| Discovery horn | yes | The coachman's horn when you discover a stop (needs Discovery effects) |
| Placement effects | yes | Road dust and a creak of tack when a stagecoach is placed near you |

The effects also follow your video settings' particle level. A server can show everyone the same travel screen; the
options then say *This server always shows the … travel screen*.

Some servers and modpacks give the road a look of their own: other landscapes, a different picture per region, their
own sounds.

## Advancements

A **Stagecoach** tab with four advancements:

| Advancement | How |
|---|---|
| On the Road | Place a stagecoach |
| Fare Paid | Finish a journey by stagecoach |
| Well Travelled | Discover 10 stagecoach stops |
| The Long Road | Finish one journey of 10,000 blocks or more |

The statistics screen counts your **Stagecoach Journeys**, **Blocks Travelled by Stagecoach**, **Stagecoach Stops
Discovered** and **Stagecoaches Placed**.

## Maps and other mods

- **JourneyMap** and **Xaero's Minimap**: the stops you know show up as a *Stagecoach* waypoint set.
- **Jade**: look at a coach to see its name, its owner, and whether you've discovered it.
- **JEI**: the recipes, and an information page on every coach.
