# Licence for modpacks

Stagecoach is open source under the **MIT licence with one condition**. In plain words:

- **You may** use it in any modpack, public or private; run it on any server; change it; fork it; use
  its code, models, textures and sounds in your own work.
- **You must** keep the licence notice with any copy you share, and credit the original author, Feelix, with a link
  to the original Stagecoach project on your download page or in your documentation.
- **Please** (a wish, not a rule): let your pack download Stagecoach and its addons from their own pages rather than
  bundling copies, so players always get the original and its updates.

There is no warranty: Stagecoach is provided as it is.

The full text is [LICENSE](https://github.com/FelixForLyfe/Stagecoach/blob/main/LICENSE). It is inside every jar too,
as `LICENSE_Stagecoach`.
