## 1.2.1 — coach stops in Luki's Grand Capitals

- **Luki's Grand Capitals** villages now get their coach stop, like every other village: its rebuilt plains, desert,
  savanna, snowy and taiga villages, each in its own wood (spruce, and dark oak in the taiga). Luki's Grand Capitals
  replaces the vanilla villages, so until now a world with it installed had no village stops at all. The mod and the
  datapack version both work.
- For Minecraft 1.20.1, 1.21.1 and 1.21.11, the versions Luki's Grand Capitals is out for. Nothing else changes, and
  1.2.0 and 1.2.1 can play together.

### On this build (Forge 1.21.1)
Works with JEI, Open Parties and Claims, JourneyMap and Xaero's Minimap when they are installed; none of them is required.
