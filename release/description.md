## How it works

1.  **Craft a stagecoach** in any of eleven woods: oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, mangrove, cherry, bamboo, crimson or warped.
2.  **Place it.** It needs a clear space 3 wide, 3 tall and 6 long, and it faces away from you. Hold one and aim at the ground to see a see-through preview: green means it fits, red means it doesn't, and anything in the way is shaded red. If it still won't place, the action bar tells you why. When you place it, you name the stop.
3.  **Discover other stops** by right-clicking them: in villages, or the ones other players built. Finding one kicks up dust and lantern embers, sounds the coachman's horn and shows the stop's name on a little road sign.
4.  **Travel.** Right-click a stop you know to open the destination list. It shows each stop's distance, cost and travel time, and you can search it by name. Pick one and you're on the road.

## The journey

*   **You pay the fare up front:** 1 level, plus 1 for every 5,000 blocks or part of that, up to 10 levels (by default). A server can charge items instead: any item from any mod, or a whole tag. Creative players travel free.
*   **The journey takes time:** 75 blocks a second, at least 5 seconds and at most 10 minutes (by default). Each player can swap the road for a map of the route, or a minimal screen.
*   **While you're on the road, you stay safe** where you set off. **Cancel at any time** to get your fare back.
*   **You arrive on safe ground** in front of the destination coach, in your own game mode.
*   **You can't set off within 10 seconds of being hit** (by default).

## How it's different from Waystones

<div style="display:flex;flex-wrap:wrap;gap:12px"><div style="width:48%;border:1px solid #444;border-radius:6px;background-color:#151515"><strong>Travel takes time</strong><br><br>Waystones teleports you. A stagecoach journey takes time: 75 blocks a second, up to 10 minutes (by default). You ride it out behind a travel screen, and you can cancel on the way to get your fare back.</div><div style="width:48%;border:1px solid #444;border-radius:6px;background-color:#151515"><strong>Physical departure points</strong><br><br>You have to stand within 12 blocks of a stagecoach to leave. There are no scrolls, stones or inventory buttons for travelling from wherever you happen to be.</div><div style="width:48%;border:1px solid #444;border-radius:6px;background-color:#151515"><strong>Stops are places</strong><br><br>Every village has one coach stop, built in the village's own wood and named to fit it ("Frostholm Crossing", "The Lucky Badger Inn"). Every other stop is built by a player, named by whoever placed it, and by default only its owner (or an operator) can take it down.</div><div style="width:48%;border:1px solid #444;border-radius:6px;background-color:#151515"><strong>A network is earned</strong><br><br>At 27 diamonds a coach, a road network is something a server builds up over time.</div><div style="width:48%;border:1px solid #444;border-radius:6px;background-color:#151515"><strong>Long roads in hops</strong><br><br>If the server sets a longest trip, far stops are reached by changing coaches on the way.</div><div style="width:48%;border:1px solid #444;border-radius:6px;background-color:#151515"><strong>No escaping a fight</strong><br><br>You can't set off within 10 seconds of being hit (by default).</div></div>

## Built to be trusted on a server

<div style="border:1px solid #444;border-radius:6px;background-color:#151515"><strong>Crash-safe</strong><br>If you quit, the server stops, or the server crashes mid-journey, you come back at the start with your fare and your game mode restored. Every journey is written to disk before anything about you changes.<br><br><strong>Server-authoritative</strong><br>The server checks every request: distance, cost, discovery and permissions.<br><br><strong>Owner-only breaking</strong> (by default)<br>Only the owner or an operator can take a coach down, and explosions can't. Village stops are locked: nobody can break them.<br><br><strong>Solid</strong><br>Every block of the coach has real collision, generated from the model itself.</div>

| --------------- |--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| <strong>FTB Ranks</strong> |The same nodes and per-player settings, straight from <code>ranks.snbt</code>. (Fabric and NeoForge, and Forge 1.20.1: FTB Ranks isn't out for Forge after 1.20.1.)     |
| <strong>KubeJS</strong> |Script events and the API from KubeJS. (1.20.1, and NeoForge 1.21.1: KubeJS isn't out for the others.)                                                                                     |
| <strong>TerraFirmaCraft</strong> |TFC recipes: lumber, steel double sheets, a gold sheet and a wooden chest. (Forge 1.20.1 and NeoForge 1.21.1, the versions TFC is out for.)                                                   |
| <strong>Sodium + Iris</strong> |Checked with both installed on Fabric 1.20.1 (no shader pack active).                                                                                                                         |
| <strong>Mod Menu</strong> |Name, description, icon and the client options (Fabric).                                                                                                                                      |

All of these are optional. Stagecoach doesn't need any of them. Jade and JEI aren't out for Forge on 1.21.11 and 26.x, so those builds go without them, and Forge 1.21.1 has JEI but not Jade.

## Configuration

`config/stagecoach.json` is created on first launch. A missing setting uses its default; an invalid one also falls back to its default, with a warning in the log. Change it while the server runs and `/stagecoach reload` picks it up.

| --------------------- |------------------- |---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <code>baseCost</code> |1                   |Levels every journey costs                                                                           |
| <code>blocksPerLevel</code> |5000                |One more level for every this many blocks (or part of that)                                          |
| <code>maxCost</code>  |10                  |The most a journey can cost                                                                          |
| <code>itemCostEnabled</code> |false               |Pay with items instead of XP levels                                                                  |
| <code>costItem</code> |<code>"minecraft:emerald"</code> |The item to pay with: any item id, vanilla or modded, or a tag like <code>"#minecraft:logs"</code>   |
| <code>itemBaseCost</code> |1                   |Items every journey costs                                                                            |
| <code>blocksPerItem</code> |1000                |One more item for every this many blocks (or part of that)                                           |
| <code>maxItemCost</code> |32                  |The most items a journey can cost                                                                    |
| <code>blocksPerSecond</code> |75                  |Travel speed                                                                                         |
| <code>minTravelSeconds</code> |5                   |Shortest journey                                                                                     |
| <code>maxTravelSeconds</code> |600                 |Longest journey                                                                                      |

## For pack makers and server owners

*   **Global stops:** an operator makes any stop global with the button on its destination list (or `/stagecoach stop flag global`): every player can then travel there from any stagecoach without discovering it. With `anyoneCanMakeGlobal` or the `stagecoach.global` node, owners can do it for their own stops.
*   **Admin commands:** create, list, rename, move ownership of, flag (global, hidden, locked, free), price and remove stops; send players on journeys; export a whole network to a file and import it on another server; change any setting in game with `/stagecoach config <setting> <value>`.
*   **Per-player settings:** any setting can be set per group or player, as LuckPerms meta or in FTB Ranks' `ranks.snbt` (`stagecoach.max_coaches_per_player`, `stagecoach.max_cost`, ...).
*   **Coaches in your own structures:** save a coach into a structure template and it becomes a stop wherever the template generates, with the name, flags, theme and wood you set.
*   **Datapacks:** travel rules that lock destinations behind an advancement, a level, an item or a permission; themes, name pools and structure injections for your own villages and towns.
*   **Resource packs:** your own travel scenes (layers or a single image), chosen by biome or dimension, and sounds.
*   **Advancement triggers and statistics** for quests (FTB Quests can use them as advancement tasks).
*   **A Java API and events**, the same on every loader.

Everything is explained in the **[pack developer guide](https://felixforlyfe.github.io/Stagecoach/pack/)** and the **[modder guide](https://felixforlyfe.github.io/Stagecoach/modders/)**, and the **[player wiki](https://felixforlyfe.github.io/Stagecoach/players/)** covers the rest.
